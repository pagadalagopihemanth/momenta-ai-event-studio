# Three-minute demonstration

This is the original Streamlit fallback script. For the current React Studio walkthrough, use [the two-minute demo](TWO_MINUTE_DEMO.md).

1. Open Guided demo and load South Indian Wedding: Bengaluru, 300 guests, INR 900,000. Explain that all data is illustrative and API-free.
2. Open Dashboard. Show the overview, budget chart, nine categories and per-guest allowance. Record an actual expense and save.
3. In Rescue & Copilot, set INR 700,000, protect Catering and Photography, and preview. Show exact totals, category savings and quotation tasks. Confirm and apply, then reopen the saved event to demonstrate persistence.
4. Ask “Reduce my decoration budget by 20%.” Show the preview and discard it to demonstrate user control. Ask “What tasks are still pending?”
5. Inspect timeline dependencies and vendor statuses. Enter a verified contact manually; emphasize that CelebrAI invents no businesses or quotations.
6. Compare 300 vs 350 guests, indoor vs outdoor, and traditional vs modern. Explain per-guest scaling and the explicitly illustrative weather allowance.
7. Open Review & Export, discuss readiness checks, and download PDF and CSV.
8. Briefly load Birthday and Community Technology Meetup to show different allocations, activities and guest experiences. Live AI is optional and requires separate approval and API billing.
